# Workout programme templates

## Foundation strength (4 weeks)
**Goal:** Build movement quality and base strength  
**Frequency:** 3x per week  
**Day A:** Goblet squat 3x10, DB RDL 3x10, Push-up 3x8-12, Row 3x10  
**Day B:** Split squat 3x8/leg, Hip thrust 3x12, DB press 3x10, Lat pulldown 3x10  
**Day C:** Trap-bar deadlift 4x6, Step-up 3x10/leg, Plank 3x45s  

## Fat loss + strength (4 weeks)
**Goal:** Maintain muscle while reducing body fat  
**Frequency:** 4x per week (2 strength, 2 conditioning)  
**Strength days:** Compound lifts 3–4 sets, 6–10 reps, 90s rest  
**Conditioning days:** Circuit 40s work / 20s rest x 4 rounds  

## Return-to-training (6 weeks)
**Goal:** Safe return after break or minor injury clearance  
**Frequency:** 2–3x per week  
**Week 1–2:** Bodyweight and light DB patterns only, RPE cap 6  
**Week 3–4:** Introduce barbell variations with submaximal loads  
**Week 5–6:** Progressive loading if pain-free and readiness ≥ 7  

## Progression rules
- Add load when all sets completed at top of rep range with RPE ≤ 8.
- If readiness < 6, reduce volume by 20% for that session.
- Re-assess movement screen every 4 weeks.
